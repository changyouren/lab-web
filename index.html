About Us

[Image Carousel — Replace with your lab photos / activities]

Welcome to the Robotics and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (RASL) at Wuhan University of Technology! Our research aims to bridge the gap between theory and practice by focusing on Artificial Intelligence, Design, Modeling, Control, and Optimization, applied to Robotic and Autonomous Systems. Our group is dedicated to advancing the frontiers of intelligent robotic systems through innovative research, collaboration, and education. We welcome motivated students and researchers to join us in making meaningful contributions.
